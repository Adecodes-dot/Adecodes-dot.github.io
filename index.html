From the lab tested, then trusted.

Research projects where the honest answer was “no”, and how I got to it.

  • Trading research · 39 written studies

    PropDesk: a backtester that judges trading ideas before any money moves.

    Each idea is tested on data it never saw and written up with a go or no-go. The lesson in numbers: a hindsight-picked portfolio showed a Sharpe ratio of 1.21; tested honestly, walk-forward, it was 0.40.

  • Football model · Python, 20,109 matches

    betpredict: can a Poisson model beat the bookmaker?

    Dixon-Coles ratings with xG, tested match by match. Verdict: no edge. Its log loss of 0.9805 trails the closing line's 0.9630, and 90% of the apparent value sat in longshots where the margin is widest.
