Pit Walla race engineer on the radio.

An offline race engineer for PS5 sim racers. It decodes live F1 25 and GT7 telemetry, learns each track from your laps, and talks to you on the straights, 71 ms (p95) from decision to sound. 291 automated tests.

The problem

Sim racers drive alone.

Real drivers have an engineer on the radio calling gaps, fuel and tyres. A solo sim racer on a PS5 has nobody, and the game's own telemetry streams past unused.

What I built

An engineer that runs on your laptop, with no internet.

Pit Wall decodes F1's UDP packets and GT7's encrypted telemetry into one schema, keeps race state, and speaks through a priority radio queue that waits for straights. Wake word and voice run offline.

Two games, one schema

F1 25 UDP and GT7's Salsa20-encrypted stream decode into the same race model.

71 ms to speech

A priority queue holds calls until a straight, then speaks within 71 ms (p95) of the decision.

Tested on real races

Every bug found in a real session became a regression test. The suite stands at 291.

What I learned

  • Synthetic test data hid menu packets, out-laps, Wi-Fi dropouts and flashbacks. Real sessions found them; regression tests keep them found.
  • A language model may rephrase a radio call, never change it: any rephrase that alters a number is rejected.
  • 37 design decisions are written down with their reasons, so the next change starts from why.
